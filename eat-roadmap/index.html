01 · Goal

The content problem is solved. The work now is picking, formatting and distributing it.

November and December turn the content already in the EAT network into clips that drive app signups, building to Thanksgiving on 26 November and Christmas.

Creators have opted in to having their past, current and future content clipped. The work now is picking the right content, putting it in a format that reads as an EAT clip, and getting it in front of as many people as possible.

07 · The EAT clip format

The creator at the centre, EAT underneath.

An EAT clip keeps the creator at the centre and puts EAT underneath, so it reads as the creator's story and never as an ad.

Formats to test first

01
The moment of giving

A creator handing out meals or helping someone, cut to the strongest few seconds.

02
List videos with a reveal

Formats like “top five things I like”, where one item points to helping people and the follow-up shows where to find them on EAT.

03
Food challenges and food talk

Existing videos from food creators, recut so the meal funds someone.

04
Creator intros

A mashup of a creator's best videos introducing them as an EAT creator, for the official page.
