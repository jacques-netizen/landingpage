The music during the campaign

Monthly listeners grew more than eight times.

“Give Me Chance (Remix)” by Winnerman featuring Davido and Sabushebu came out on 16 October 2026, the day the clips started. Since then, Winnerman's Spotify monthly listeners grew more than eight times, and the song picked up 248,124 Spotify streams. These numbers show what moved and when, next to when the clips went out. They do not show how much of the growth came from the clips, since other things, such as the artists' own posts, also move a song.

What we learned

Five things this campaign showed.

Large pages gave the widest reach

Two placements on one major London culture page brought 2.3M views across Instagram and Facebook, about 85% of the total. Large pages gave the song its widest reach.

TikTok carried the volume

104 clips went out there, many of them from lyric pages, which kept the song in circulation for the whole campaign.

The TikTok result was spread out

The strongest TikTok clip reached 54,725 views. Most TikTok clips stayed under 10,000 views, so the TikTok result came from many clips, not from one hit.

The song grew while the campaign ran

The biggest rise in daily streams came on 10 to 13 November, not in the days right after the two biggest posts. The data cannot show where that rise came from.

Count from day one

TikTok and Shazam were only counted from 18 November, so this report shows their totals but not how they moved during the campaign. Next time, both are counted from the first day.
