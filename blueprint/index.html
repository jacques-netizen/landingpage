The first thing Claude says when you paste the prompt.

    Your sport, your level, the turning points and the setbacks, injuries included.

  2. 2

    Problems solved

    Results and numbers, and what teammates, younger players and family already ask you for help with.

    Three offer options built from your own answers, a price test, and an honest verdict on whether it can sell for $2,000 or more.
