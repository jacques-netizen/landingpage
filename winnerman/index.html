02 · The idea

The fastest discovery engine in Nigerian music already exists.

It is the Davido fanbase. OBO, 30BG, and the meme, music and entertainment pages that live around it move millions of views every week, to exactly the audience Winnerman needs: young Nigerians who already listen to Afrobeats and act on what they hear.

We put Winnerman's songs into that stream. Creators on those pages cut fan edits, reaction clips and trend-led content with Winnerman's music carrying them. Every post tags @winnermanuniverse and uses the agreed hashtags and sound, so the attention does not stay on the page. It turns into follows and streams.

05 · Clipping setup

Paid on verified views, and nothing else.

Payout

Creators are paid per verified view. No views, no pay. The budget goes into reach rather than into fees for posts that go nowhere.

Rules
  • The @winnermanuniverse tag is required
  • A minimum quality bar on every clip
  • No botted views
  • No deleting posts early
Tracking

Creators submit their links, we verify the views, and payouts go out weekly. Anything that fails verification is not paid.
