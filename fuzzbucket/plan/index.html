How it runs

Creators run real campaigns in Fuzzbucket, from Brand Brain to strategy to finished creative, so every video shows the product doing an agency's actual job.

Clippers post across four platforms. They only get paid once a clip passes 10K views, and pay stops at 250K views per clip. Every view after that is free.
