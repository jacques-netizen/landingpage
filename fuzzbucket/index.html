The engine

How the reach gets manufactured.

Distribution runs on a performance-paid clipper network, moderated and measured from the first clip to the last.

  • Floor

    Nothing is paid on a clip below 10,000 views. Below that line, it does not count.

  • Ceiling

    Payout is capped at 250,000 views per clip. Beyond that, the clip keeps running, the cost does not keep climbing.

  • Moderation

    Every clip is reviewed before it is paid, and every payment carries a clawback if the views behind it are not real.

  • Visibility

    A live dashboard shows the campaign as it runs. Nothing is reported after the fact that was not visible during it.

Guaranteed, stated up front

Every campaign carries a guaranteed view floor, set before a dollar moves. Section 05 states the number for this one.
