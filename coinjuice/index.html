03 · Why The Ads Did Not Work

Roughly $3,000 into Meta since February.

Two reasons, not one, and neither is a product problem.

Reason one

Paid ads solicit a stranger and ask for money before trust exists. There is no relationship for the ad to draw on. It asks a cold audience to pay on the strength of a headline.

Reason two

There is no credibility surface for a hesitant buyer to check. The credentials are real: the crypto site grown to roughly a million clicks a month, the CoinDesk invitation, the years in the industry. None of it is visible at the point of purchase.

06 · The Structure

What the first quarter is really for.

Two phases. The first is not just views, it identifies which editors actually drive leads. The second puts them on a structure weighted to that result.

Phase one

Find the editors who convert attention into leads

Campaign budget plus retainer, the retainer split on signature and on results. Distribution runs across the network, and every editor's performance is visible from day one.

Phase two

Put them on a CoinJuice specific structure

The top performing editors move to a dedicated CoinJuice clipping team, paid on an affiliate, conversion weighted basis. Effective CPM drops across the quarter as a result.

  • CPM only Editors are paid on CPM performance only, out of the campaign budget. Never from the retainer.
  • 10,000 view floor A post must reach 10,000 views to qualify for payment. Everything below that line is free reach.
  • 250,000 view cap Payout is capped at 250,000 views per post. Everything above that is free reach.
  • 30 day window The guarantee window runs 30 days, and is typically expended in two to three weeks.
