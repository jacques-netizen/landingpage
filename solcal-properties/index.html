02 · The idea

Creators do the giving. SolCal gets the thanks.

The campaign runs with EAT, the network behind Everybody Eats, where creators make videos that feed people. Creators film real acts of giving: buying meals, feeding neighbors, helping a family stretch a food budget. In each video the creator thanks SolCal Properties for making it happen.

Our clippers then cut the best videos into short clips and post them across TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, so each moment is seen many more times than the creator's own post.

SolCal funds it
One budget covers the creators and the clipping

SolCal does not have to film anything or appear in any video. The creators do the work, and SolCal gets the credit.

06 · Clipping setup

Paid for real views, after they are checked.

Payout

Clippers are paid per view, and only after their views are checked. Posts that go nowhere cost nothing, so the budget goes into reach.

Rules
  • SolCal's name and license number on screen
  • A quality bar on every clip
  • No fake or bought views
  • No deleting posts early
Checks

Clippers send in their links and every post is checked for real views and real engagement. Anything that fails the check is not paid.
