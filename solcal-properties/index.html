02 · The idea

Every listing becomes a reason to help.

SolCal wants more people looking at its listings, and it wants to give back through the end of the year. This campaign does both in the same video. Each home gets one simple ask: help it enter escrow in 30 days, and a set donation goes to ending hunger.

Anyone watching can help: share the home, comment, or send it to someone who is looking. The story stays about helping people, with the home at the centre of it.

    07 · Clipping setup

    Paid for real views, after they are checked.

    Payout

    Clippers are paid per view, and only after their views are checked. Posts that go nowhere cost nothing, so the budget goes into reach.

    Rules
    • The 30-day ask in every clip
    • SolCal's name and license number on screen
    • No fake or bought views
    • No deleting posts early
    Checks

    Clippers send in their links and every post is checked for real views and real engagement. Anything that fails the check is not paid.
