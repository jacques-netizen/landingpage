The automations: no interested person slips through

Instagram · DMs

Someone comments a keyword on a post and gets an instant DM with the free resource. A few short questions find out what they need, then send the right link: the offer or a booking page.

Four viewers already did this on In Five Years. From week 3 every one of them is caught and followed up.

DM flow

Lead tracking

Follow-up reminders

People who opened a link but did not buy get a nudge a day or two later.

The content engine, from week 1

The rule for next month is simple: do more of what already works.

Start from winners

The best-performing videos are reposted as trial reels with a small change, to reach new people.

Scripts

Each new video is scripted from proven formats, with a hook in the first line and one clear action at the end.

Editing

A dedicated editor turns raw footage into finished videos in a consistent style.

Rhythm

Film in batches once or twice a week, so posting never stops.

Review

Every week: views, shares, follows and leads per video. Keep the formats that convert.
