# Film scripts (drafts for the owner)

Drafts for the founder takes in appendix A of the build plan. Plain words, no
claims outside `content/proof.json`, no personal names. Read them in your own
voice; the exact wording can change, but keep the claims the same.

Format for every take: vertical 9:16, 1080 x 1920, H.264 mp4, under 60 MB,
plus a separate `.vtt` caption file. Upload, then edit `content/media.json`
(provider, id, poster, captions, `placeholder: false`). Nothing else changes.

## V1 Chapter 1, founder (60 to 90 seconds)

> You found us through a clip. That is the whole idea.
>
> Clipping is simple. You take the best moments from longer content, a song,
> a show, a stream, a launch, and cut them into short videos.
>
> Then lots of people post those clips on their own accounts. TikTok, Reels,
> Shorts, X.
>
> Why does it work? People scroll past ads. They stop for clips. A clip feels
> like something someone shared, not something someone bought.
>
> And when the same song or product keeps showing up, from different accounts,
> people notice. Then they go looking for it.
>
> That is what we do at Maison d'Élites. We run a network of clippers who
> choose the campaigns they want to post for. Every post is checked before it
> counts, and brands only pay for the views that pass review.
>
> If you want to clip, that network is where you come in.
>
> Over the next few minutes I will show you real campaigns, ask you a few
> questions, and build you a guide from your answers. Let's go.

Cutaways: campaign clips on phones, the review step, a feed scrolling.
