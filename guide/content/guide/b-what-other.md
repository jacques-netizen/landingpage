title: What clipping does for your content
order: 1
---
Clipping takes the best moments from your content and turns them into short videos. Clippers post those videos on their own accounts, across TikTok, Reels, Shorts and X.

It works because people scroll past ads, but they stop for clips. A clip feels like something someone shared, not something someone bought. When the same content keeps showing up, from many different accounts, people notice. Then they go looking for it.

The question to ask about your content is simple: which thirty seconds would make a stranger stop scrolling? Those moments are what a campaign is built on.
