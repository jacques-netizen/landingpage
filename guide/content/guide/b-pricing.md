Clipping is priced on results, so it helps to know the words.

**CPM** means cost per 1,000 views. If a campaign pays $2.00 per 1,000 counted views, then 100,000 counted views cost $200. **Effective CPM** is what you actually paid per 1,000 counted views once the campaign is done. It is the number to compare against other ways of buying attention.

### The two parts of a campaign

**The work.** A fee covers what we run on our side: strategy, briefing clippers, review and reporting.

**The clipping budget.** This pays for the views themselves, at a rate per 1,000 views, with a cap on how much any one clip can earn. The cap keeps spend predictable and stops one clip from using the whole budget.

### Two ways to clip

**Mass clipping** uses a large pool of clippers for volume: many accounts, many clips, fast. It suits releases, launches and big moments where being everywhere at once is the point.

**A managed clipper team** is smaller and closer to you. It suits campaigns where you want people to take an action, like buying or signing up.

Larger spend commitments unlock lower fees. We size the fee and the budget to your goal on the call.
