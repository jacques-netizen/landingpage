title: A day of clipping
order: 1
---
**1. Read the brief.** Note the rate, the cap, what must be in every clip and what is not allowed.

**2. Find the moments.** Watch or listen to the source content and write down the times of every strong moment: a line, a reaction, a drop, a turn.

**3. Cut several versions.** For your best moments, try two or three different hooks. Small changes in the first second can change everything.

**4. Post at good times.** Spread posts out across the day and across your accounts rather than posting everything at once.

**5. Check the numbers.** After a day, look at which clips kept people watching. Make more of what worked.

**6. Submit and track.** Make sure each post is submitted the way the campaign asks, so it can be reviewed and counted.
