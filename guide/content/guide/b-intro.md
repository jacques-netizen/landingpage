Clipping is simple. You take the best moments from longer content (a song, a show, a stream, a launch video, an interview) and cut them into short videos. Then many people post those clips on their own accounts, across TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and X.

It started with big creators paying people to repost their clips. Today labels, brands and TV shows use it to put their content in front of people who would never see it otherwise.

### Why it works

**People skip ads. They stop for clips.** An ad looks like an ad, so thumbs move past it. A clip looks like something someone shared, so people watch it.

**The feed does the finding.** Short video apps show clips to people based on what they like to watch, not only on who they follow. A clip from an account with few followers can still reach strangers who love that kind of content.

**Repetition builds memory.** One post is easy to forget. The same song, face or product showing up again and again, from different accounts, in different feeds, is not. After enough times, people go looking for it.

**You pay for results, not promises.** You pay for views that pass review, not for a post going live and hoping it lands.
