You set the goal. We run the network. There are four steps.

**1. Brief and strategy.** You work with our team to set your niche, your content, the rate per 1,000 views and the budget. The brief says what clippers can post, what they cannot, and what every clip must include, such as your sound, your handle or a link.

**2. Clippers activate.** Your campaign goes live to the network. Clippers choose it based on fit and audience, so the people posting are the ones whose followers fit your content. The network is not a fixed roster. It is a network of clippers who pick the campaigns that suit them, which is why it can reach many niches at once.

**3. Review before it counts.** Every post is checked before it counts, and every approved post links to its source. Clips that break the brief do not count.

**4. Pay for counted views.** You pay only for views that pass review. Posts keep working after the budget is spent.

### What we handle, and what you handle

We handle clipper outreach, briefing, review and payouts. You give us the content, agree the brief, and make sure the place people land on is ready. You follow everything on a live dashboard with a link to every approved post.
