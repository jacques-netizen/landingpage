You set the goal. We run the network. There are four steps.

**Brief and strategy.** You work with our team to set your niche, your content, the rate per 1,000 views and the budget.

**Clippers activate.** Your campaign goes live to the network. Clippers choose it based on fit and audience, so the people posting are the ones whose followers fit your content.

**Review before it counts.** Every post is checked before it counts, and every approved post links to its source.

**Pay for counted views.** You pay only for views that pass review. Posts keep working after the budget is spent.

The network is not a fixed roster. It is a network of clippers who pick the campaigns that suit them, which is why it can reach many niches at once.
