title: What to hand clippers
order: 1
---
The campaign is only as good as the material clippers get. Before launch, put these together.

**Source content.** The full videos, the song, the episodes, the footage. More raw material means more different clips. Long content is fine; clippers find the moments.

**The moments you love.** Point to the parts you think are strongest. Clippers will find others, but your picks give them a head start.

**The rules.** What must be in every clip (your sound, your handle, a link, a hashtag), what must never be in one, and anything sensitive to avoid.

**The goal.** Views, streams, sales or awareness. Clippers edit differently for each, so tell them which one matters.

**A landing place.** Where people should go after the clip: a profile, a song, a store, a page. Check it works on a phone.
