title: Picking music campaigns
order: 1
---
Music campaigns pay for views on clips that use the track. The best clips make people want to hear the song again: a lyric that hits, a live moment, a dance, a reaction.

**Use the official sound.** Most music briefs ask for it, because every clip adds to the song's count on the platform.

**Lead with the hook.** Start on the part of the song people will remember.

**Pick songs you would play.** Choose campaigns in the genre your accounts already post. Your followers will tell you fast whether a sound fits.
