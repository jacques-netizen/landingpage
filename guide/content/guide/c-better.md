title: Getting better, clip by clip
order: 1
---
Every clip is a test. The numbers tell you what to change.

**Watch the first seconds.** If most people leave early, the hook is the problem. Try starting later in the moment, or put a caption on screen straight away.

**Watch completion.** If people leave halfway, the clip is too long. Cut harder.

**Copy your own winners.** When a clip does well, make more like it: same kind of moment, same style of hook, same length.

**Change one thing at a time,** so you know what made the difference.
