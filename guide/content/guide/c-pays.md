Each campaign sets a rate per 1,000 views. You get paid for the views that pass review, at that rate.

### A worked example

Campaigns in the network have paid $2.00 per 1,000 views. At that rate, a clip with 50,000 counted views earns $100. A clip with 5,000 counted views earns a tenth of that.

### What decides what you earn

**The rate.** Rates are set per campaign, so they change from one campaign to the next.

**Counted views.** Only views on posts that pass review are paid. A big clip that breaks the brief earns nothing.

**The cap.** Campaigns can set a maximum payout per clip, so one clip cannot earn without limit. Check the cap before you plan around one big clip.

**How many good clips you post.** Most views come from a few clips that take off. More good attempts means more chances.

Nobody can promise you an income from clipping, and you should be careful of anyone who does. What you earn depends on your clips and the campaigns you pick.
