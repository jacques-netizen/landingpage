title: How clipping pays
order: 1
---
A brand, artist or show starts a campaign and sets a rate per 1,000 views. You make clips from their content, post them on your own accounts, and get paid for the views that pass review.

Three things decide what you earn: the rate the campaign sets, how many of your views pass review, and how many campaigns you work. Rates are set per campaign, so they change from one campaign to the next.

Nobody can promise you an income from clipping, and you should be careful of anyone who does. What you earn depends on your clips and the campaigns you pick.
