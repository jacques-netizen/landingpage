title: What clipping does for a personal brand
order: 1
---
People follow people they keep seeing. Your own account only reaches the people who already follow you. Clipping puts you in front of everyone else.

Clippers take your best moments, from videos, talks, interviews or streams, and post them as short clips on their own accounts. New people see you again and again, in different places, until your name and face feel familiar.

That is how a personal brand grows: not from one viral post, but from being seen often by the right people.
