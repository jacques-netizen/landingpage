title: The rules that keep you paid
order: 1
---
**Never pretend to be an official page.** Do not use names, logos or photos that make people think you are the artist, brand or show.

**Follow each platform's rules.** Accounts that break them get limited or banned, and views on a banned account do not count.

**Say when content is paid.** Where the rules require it, label paid posts clearly.

**Follow the brief.** Every post is checked before it counts. A clip that breaks the brief is not paid.
