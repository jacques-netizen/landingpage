Sports and gaming fans already share highlights. A goal, a clutch play or a big reaction can travel far on its own. Clipping makes sure your moments are the ones travelling.

Clippers cut your best plays and reactions into short videos and post them on their own accounts, often within hours. Fans see them in the feeds they already scroll, then come looking for the full match, stream or channel.

### What makes highlight clips work

**Speed.** A highlight is worth the most in the first hours after it happens. The sooner footage reaches clippers, the more the moment works for you.

**Reaction as well as action.** The play is half of it. The commentator, the crowd or the streamer's face is often what people share.

**Fan language.** Clips made by people who follow the sport or the game sound right to fans. Clippers choose campaigns in niches they know.
