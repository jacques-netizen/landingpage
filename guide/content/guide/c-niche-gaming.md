title: Picking gaming campaigns
order: 1
---
Gaming clips win or lose in the first second: the clutch play, the fail, the reaction. Viewers know the games, so getting the details right matters.

**Show the streamer's face** when the moment lands. Reactions travel.

**Use the game's language** in captions. Fans can tell when a clip was made by someone who plays.

**Pick games and streamers you know,** so your clips sound like a fan made them.
