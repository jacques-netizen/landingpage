You have clipped before, so focus on the set up that campaigns check.

**Keep accounts clean.** One niche per account, a clear name and photo, and nothing that looks like an official page.

**Keep a record.** Note which clips did well and why: the hook, the audio, the length. That is how you pick the next campaign.

**Check each brief.** Every campaign has its own rules about what to post, where and how. Clips that break the brief do not count.
