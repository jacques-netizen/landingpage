You have clipped before, so focus on the set up that campaigns check.

**Keep accounts clean.** One niche per account, a clear name and photo, and nothing that looks like an official page.

**Keep a record.** For every clip, note the campaign, the hook, the length, the audio and the views after a day and a week. After a month that record tells you more than any guide.

**Check each brief.** Every campaign has its own rules about what to post, where and how. Clips that break the brief do not count, however well they do.

**Build a system.** A folder per campaign, a list of moments to cut, and set times to post. Clipping rewards people who are consistent.
