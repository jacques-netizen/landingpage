Taking any campaign that pays sounds smart, but it is usually slower. Accounts grow when they post one kind of clip, and you get better at what you repeat.

**Start with one or two campaigns** that fit the accounts you already have.

**Compare rate and fit,** not rate alone. A lower rate on a campaign your audience loves can pay more than a high rate nobody watches.

**Add more once those are working.**
