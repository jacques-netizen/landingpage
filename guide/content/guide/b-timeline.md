order: 1
---
**Before launch.** The brief is agreed and the content is handed over. This is where most campaigns are won or lost.

**The first 72 hours.** Clips go live and views start moving. Early numbers show which moments and which hooks are working.

**The weeks after.** This is where much of the value sits. Clips do not expire. They keep getting found, so views keep adding up long after launch, and posts keep working after the budget is spent.

**Reading the results.** Most clients run a defined first campaign, long enough to read the numbers honestly, then decide whether to scale. Judge it on counted views, effective CPM and what happened to your own numbers (streams, sales, followers), not on the first day alone.
