title: What clipping does for a product launch
order: 1
---
A launch has a short window. People need to see the product many times, from many places, while it is new.

Clippers put the product into short videos on their own accounts: unboxings, reactions, the moment it does something useful. It looks like something people share, not something someone bought, so people watch it instead of skipping it.

Because many clippers post at the same time, the product shows up across feeds during launch week instead of one post at a time.
