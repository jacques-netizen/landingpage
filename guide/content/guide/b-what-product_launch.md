A launch has a short window. People need to see the product many times, from many places, while it is new.

Clippers put the product into short videos on their own accounts: unboxings, reactions, the moment it does something useful, the first try. It looks like something people share, not something someone bought, so people watch it instead of skipping it.

Because many clippers post at the same time, the product shows up across feeds during launch week instead of one post at a time.

### What makes product clips work

**Show it doing something.** A clip of the product in use beats a clip of the product on a table.

**Make the next step easy.** Clips create the attention. Your page, link or store has to catch it, and it has to load fast on a phone.

**Give clippers real footage.** The more raw material they have (demos, reactions, close ups), the more different clips they can make.
