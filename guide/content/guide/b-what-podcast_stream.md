title: What clipping does for a podcast or stream
order: 1
---
A long show is full of short moments: a story, a hot take, a laugh, a clash. Most people will never press play on a two hour episode. But they will watch thirty seconds of the best part.

Clippers find those moments and post them as short videos on their own accounts. Each clip is a small door into the full show. The people who walk through it are new listeners and viewers who came because a moment caught them, not because an ad did.

The more good moments are out there, the more doors there are.
