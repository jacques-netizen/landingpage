A long show is full of short moments: a story, a strong opinion, a laugh, an argument. Most people will never press play on a two hour episode. But they will watch thirty seconds of the best part.

Clippers find those moments and post them as short videos on their own accounts. Each clip is a small door into the full show. The people who walk through it are new listeners and viewers who came because a moment caught them, not because an ad did.

### What makes show clips work

**A moment that stands alone.** The best clips make sense to someone who has never seen the show. If it needs context, it needs a caption that gives it.

**Captions on screen.** Many people watch with the sound off. Words on screen keep them watching.

**Volume.** A two hour episode can give dozens of clips. The more good moments are out there, the more doors there are into the show.
