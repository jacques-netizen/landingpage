Clipping means taking the best moments from longer content (a song, a show, a stream, a game) and turning them into short videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and X.

Artists, brands and shows pay for this, because clips put their content in front of people who would never see it otherwise. That is where you come in.

### How a campaign works

**A campaign goes live.** An artist, brand or show sets a brief, a rate per 1,000 views and a budget.

**You pick it.** You choose campaigns that fit your niche and your audience. Nobody assigns them to you.

**You post on your own accounts.** You make clips from the source content and post them, following the brief.

**Every post is checked.** Posts are reviewed before they count. Views on approved posts are the views you get paid for.

You need a phone, an editing app and the patience to learn what makes people stop scrolling. That last part is the real skill, and the rest of this guide is about it.
