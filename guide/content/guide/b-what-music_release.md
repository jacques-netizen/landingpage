title: What clipping does for a music release
order: 1
---
A song grows when people hear it again and again, in many places, in a short window. One post from one account is easy to scroll past. The same song under twenty different clips, on twenty different accounts, is hard to miss.

That is what clipping does for a release. Clippers cut short videos that use your track: a live moment, a lyric, a dance, a reaction. They post them on their own accounts, where the feed shows them to people who have never heard of you.

When people hear the hook enough times, they go looking for it. That is when a release starts to move on its own.
