title: Five mistakes that sink campaigns
order: 1
---
**Weak source content.** Clippers cannot make a strong clip from a weak moment. Give them your best material, not what is left over.

**Nowhere to land.** Attention with nowhere to go is wasted. Have the song live, the store working or the profile ready before day one.

**Rules nobody can follow.** A brief with too many must haves makes clips stiff, and stiff clips do not travel. Keep the must haves short.

**Judging too early.** Day one is not the result. Clips keep picking up views for weeks.

**One clip, one try.** Campaigns work through volume and variety: many clips, many hooks, many accounts. Let the numbers show which ones win.
