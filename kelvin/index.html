02 · The real problem

This is a branding and distribution problem. Port Harcourt is not the problem.

Burna Boy came out of Port Harcourt. What holds you back is that people do not know who you are yet, so there is nothing for a label to invest in. Music is a business. A label signs an artist when it can see the fans, the ticket sales and a brand it can sell. Right now your page mostly shows other artists featuring you.

03 · The goal

Six months to become the artist labels want to work with.

You set the bar on the call: 13 to 15 million streams. Two to three years is how long it usually takes an artist to break. The six months below are about getting the foundations right so every release from here moves you toward that number.

04 · The roadmap

Your plan, put in the right order.

The pieces you mentioned on the call are the right ones: content in the city, creators using your sound, the December shows, Abuja and Lagos. Done in order, each step makes the next one land harder.
