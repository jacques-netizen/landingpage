01 · Why this, why now

Chillblast is moving from PC builder to premium lifestyle brand.

These are products people want to be seen with. A brand like that is built on how often people see it, in what company, and from whom.

Aston Martin collectionThe LabCryo rangeSim racing rigs

Attention for Chillblast's buyers now lives on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. The brands winning there have moved past the single official account. They either pay hundreds of independent pages to post about them, or run their own networks of pages that post every day. Both are bought on performance, and both cost a fraction of paid social.

05 · Model A · Reach

Chillblast pays only for views verified as real.

Maison d'Élites selects pages from its network and brings in a small group of named creators. They post short videos centred on Chillblast products on their own accounts. A clip nobody watches costs nothing.

A curated campaign

Most clipping campaigns let anyone join. That suits a streamer and does not suit a premium brand. We choose every page, Chillblast sees the list, and every clip is reviewed before it is paid.

06 · How we vet the pages

How a page gets in, and how it stays in.

Brand rules, signed by every page

The product is the subject of the clip.
Chillblast named correctly, with the required tag and handle on every post.
No political, adult, gambling or controversial content on the page during the campaign.
No competitor hardware as the hero of a clip.
No invented claims, fake discounts or made-up specs.
No AI-generated product footage.
Clear paid-content disclosure on every post, in line with UK advertising rules.
Any clip taken down within 24 hours of Chillblast's request.
