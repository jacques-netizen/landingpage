01 · The Engagement

One team, running the engine.

Maison d’Élites runs ValuePilot’s growth as its marketing engine, with Jacques Amoako as EVP Marketing leading it. MDE brings an in-house team, a creator and clipping network, and a proven distribution system.

The Company funds a marketing budget the EVP deploys, with full transparency and reporting. That budget is separate from the retainer that pays for the team and the system. There are two ways to structure the retainer, shown in Section 08 below.

04 · The Engine

Transparency is the sell.

How reach is manufactured, in plain terms.

  • 01

    Hero content is produced

    Jacques is the primary face. External creators are capped at $500 per long-form piece, to keep this lean.

  • 02

    Every long-form asset is cut into 30 to 50 short clips

    One source, dozens of assets.

  • 03

    A performance-paid clipper network posts at volume

    Paid $1 to $2 per 1,000 views delivered. This is the cheapest reach available and the core of the model.

  • 04

    Winning clips get paid amplification

    Attention is captured to an owned email list and converted from there.

Strategic note

The clipping spend builds toward an in-house ValuePilot clipping team. The budget creates an owned distribution asset, not rented reach that disappears when spending stops.
