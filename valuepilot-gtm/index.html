02 · Research Before Reach

We do not guess. We study the winners first.

Before a single piece of content is made, every candidate product gets a full competitor and market teardown. Four questions, answered in writing:

Who they are

The direct and adjacent competitors for each product, mapped.

How they marketed

Exactly which channels they used, which influencer and creator partnerships they ran, which content formats and hooks actually worked.

Where the audiences live

Which platforms and communities their users sit in, and what they respond to.

What they missed

The gaps: angles and channels competitors underused that we can own.

Output

A written teardown document per product, plus a channel and creator shortlist. This is week one and week two work, and it is what the brand and content strategy stands on.

04 · The Four Pillars

Four ways in. One starting point.

Every acquisition motion falls into one of four pillars. All four have a role, but they are not equal at the start. Tap each pillar.

Warm Outreach
+

Relationships already within reach: communities, existing contacts, early believers. Warm outreach supports the partnership and B2B tracks, where a trusted introduction does the heavy lifting. Role: supporting.

Cold Outreach
+

Opening doors that do not know us yet: partnership prospects, community operators, and later the B2B pipeline for LeakageIndex. Precise and personal, never mass spam. Role: supporting.

Paid Advertising
+

Comes later by design, and cheaper. Not a dollar of ad spend until organic content has proven which creative converts. Then paid distribution travels further, because we only boost what people already demonstrated they like. Role: later, on proof.

Start here
Content
+
  • Awareness is the first battle. It does not matter how good the product is if nobody knows or trusts it.
  • Influencers transfer trust fastest. Creators relevant to each product’s niche move awareness and trust faster than any other channel. Food creators for GrubBudget, finance creators for RenewalGuard and RefundPilot.
  • Scale compounds. The bigger the creator, the more awareness and trust it lends the brand.
  • Creators produce both forms. Short form and long form: podcasts, live streams, YouTube challenges, reviews. One relationship, many assets.
05 · The Distribution Engine

The awareness machine.

Six stages. Each one feeds the next.

01

Source

Niche-relevant creators produce hero content, short form and long form: podcasts, streams, challenges, reviews.

02

Multiply

Every long-form piece is cut into dozens of short clips.

03

Redistribute

The clipping and distribution network pushes the clips at mass scale, targeting millions upon millions of views.

04

Capture

Trackers and CRM are set up from day one, so the first wave of attention converts into signups and emails, not just views.

05

Learn

The first wave tells us which content pieces and formats actually convert.

06

Promote from within

The best performers in the distribution network transition into the in-house content distributor team, promoting on their own pages on an affiliate basis.

Free first

We launch on a free tier because we are in the awareness stage. Trust before monetization. The email list and the product experience do the converting later.

06 · Owning the Audience

The list is the asset.

Collect
Every campaign collects emails

Views are the spark. Names on a list are the asset. Every campaign, creator, and clip routes attention toward capture.

Deplatform
Rented reach becomes owned reach

Views on rented platforms become names on an owned list, nurtured by email sequences: waitlist, education, launch, onboarding.

Attribute
CRM and trackers stitched through everything

Every creator, clip, and campaign is attributed. We always know what produced what.

07 · Paid Ads, Later and Cheaper

The sequence is the strategy.

Organic first, paid second. Because the winning formats are proven organically before a dollar of ad spend, the cost of acquisition drops sharply: we only boost creative that people already demonstrated they like, so paid distribution travels further.
